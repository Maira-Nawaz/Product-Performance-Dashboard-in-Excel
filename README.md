# Product-Performance-Dashboard-in-Excel
 

This dataset provides a **comprehensive analysis of product performance** for the fiscal year **2023-2024**, covering three key aspects:  

### **1️⃣ Sales Distribution Across Products**  
- Contains sales data for multiple product IDs.  
- Metrics included:  
  - **Mean Sales**: Average sales value for each product.  
  - **Median Sales**: Central sales value to reduce the effect of outliers.  
  - **Max Sales**: Highest recorded sales for each product.  
  - **Min Sales**: Lowest recorded sales for each product.  
  - **Quartiles (Q1 & Q3)**: Measures of distribution to understand variability in sales.  

### **2️⃣ Profit Margin Across Products**  
- Represents the profitability of each product based on sales and cost.  
- **PROD_ID_01** has the **lowest profit margin**, indicating low profitability.  
- **PROD_ID_06** has the **highest profit margin**, suggesting a high return on investment.  
- Used to evaluate pricing strategies and optimize product profitability.  

### **3️⃣ Customer Satisfaction Across Products**  
- Captures customer feedback across five categories:  
  - **Very Satisfied** 😊  
  - **Satisfied** 🙂  
  - **Neutral** 😐  
  - **Dissatisfied** ☹️  
  - **Very Dissatisfied** 😡  
- Provides insights into customer sentiment for each product.  
- Helps in identifying improvement areas for products with low satisfaction ratings.  

This dataset enables **data-driven decision-making** for **sales optimization, pricing strategy, and customer experience enhancement**.
